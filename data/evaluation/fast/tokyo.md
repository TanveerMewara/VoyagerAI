# Itinerary

*   **Day 1 (Arrival):**
    *   Morning: Shinkansen from Osaka to Tokyo, check into hotel.
    *   Afternoon: Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.
    *   Evening: Dinner in Shinjuku, enjoy views from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building.
*   **Day 2 (Culture & History):**
    *   Morning: Visit Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise-dori in Asakusa.
    *   Afternoon: Explore Ueno Park, visit Tokyo National Museum.
    *   Evening: Dinner in Ueno or Ginza, enjoy a traditional izakaya experience.
*   **Day 3 (Modern Tokyo & Parks):**
    *   Morning: Wander through Harajuku (Takeshita Street, Meiji Jingu Shrine).
    *   Afternoon: Relax in Yoyogi Park.
    *   Evening: Explore Shibuya Crossing and dinner in Shibuya.
*   **Day 4 (Food & Views):**
    *   Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market for fresh seafood breakfast/lunch.
    *   Afternoon: Explore Ginza's upscale shops or visit the Imperial Palace East Garden.
    *   Evening: Dinner in Ginza or a themed restaurant experience.
*   **Day 5 (Departure):**
    *   Morning: Last-minute souvenir shopping or revisit a favorite spot.
    *   Afternoon: Lunch near Tokyo Station, take Shinkansen back to Osaka.
    *   Evening: Arrive in Osaka.

# Travel Tips

1.  **Get a Suica/Pasmo card:** Essential for seamless public transport on trains and subways.
2.  **Book Shinkansen tickets in advance:** Especially for popular routes like Tokyo to Osaka.
3.  **Check opening times:** Verify current operating hours for specific attractions and restaurants online.
4.  **Carry cash for smaller establishments:** While cards are widely accepted, some smaller eateries prefer cash.
5.  **Utilize Google Maps for navigation:** Excellent for public transport directions and walking routes.
