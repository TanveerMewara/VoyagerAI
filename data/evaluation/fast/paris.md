# Itinerary
*   **Day 1 (Arrival & Louvre):** Morning Eurostar London to Paris; check into hotel. Afternoon Louvre Museum (pre-booked ticket). Evening Dinner in Le Marais.
*   **Day 2 (Culture & Cuisine):** Morning Musée d'Orsay (pre-booked ticket). Afternoon explore Saint-Germain-des-Prés, Notre Dame exterior. Evening Seine River cruise and traditional French dinner.
*   **Day 3 (Art & Departure):** Morning Montmartre exploration, Sacré-Cœur Basilica. Afternoon visit a smaller gallery (e.g., Musée de l'Orangerie) or last-minute souvenir shopping. Evening Eurostar Paris to London.

# Travel Tips

1.  **Pre-book attractions:** Secure tickets for popular museums (Louvre, Orsay) well in advance to save time.
2.  **Utilize the Metro:** Paris's metro system is efficient and easy to navigate for getting around.
3.  **Learn basic French phrases:** A few words like "Bonjour," "Merci," and "S'il vous plaît" go a long way.
4.  **Check opening times:** Verify current operating hours for museums and restaurants before visiting.
5.  **Wear comfortable shoes:** You'll do a lot of walking exploring Paris's charming streets and museums.
