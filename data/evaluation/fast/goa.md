# Itinerary
*   **Day 1 (Arrival in Goa):**
    *   **Morning:** Arrive Mopa Airport (GOX) from Mumbai, transfer to North Goa accommodation.
    *   **Afternoon:** Relax at Anjuna Beach, enjoy a beachfront shack lunch.
    *   **Evening:** Explore Anjuna Flea Market (if operational), followed by dinner at a local Goan restaurant.
*   **Day 2 (Departure from Goa):**
    *   **Morning:** Visit Vagator Beach and Chapora Fort for scenic views.
    *   **Afternoon:** Indulge in a final Goan seafood lunch, souvenir shopping.
    *   **Evening:** Transfer to Mopa Airport (GOX) for departure to Mumbai.

# Hotel Recommendations

*   **W Goa (Approx. INR 20000/night):** Luxury beachfront resort with excellent amenities for a splurge.
*   **Radisson Resort Goa Baga (Approx. INR 8000/night):** Well-located with good facilities, close to Baga Beach.
*   **Goa Cottages Arpora (Approx. INR 4000/night):** Budget-friendly, clean stay with a pool, slightly away from the busiest areas.

# Travel Tips

1.  Rent a scooter or car for convenient local transport, but drive carefully.
2.  Try local Goan specialties like Xacuti, Vindaloo, and fresh seafood curries.
3.  Hydrate frequently, especially on beaches, and use high-SPF sunscreen.
4.  Bargain politely at local markets for better deals on souvenirs.
5.  Check current opening times for specific shacks or attractions, as they can vary seasonally.
