This is a wonderful time to visit Paris – pleasant and comfortable for exploring!

### Day 1: Arrival & Artistic Immersion

*   **Morning (09:00 - 12:00):** Take the Eurostar from London St Pancras to Paris Gare du Nord (approx. 2.5 hours).
*   **Late Morning (12:00 - 13:00):** Arrive in Paris, check into your hotel, and drop off luggage.
*   **Lunch (13:00 - 14:00):** Enjoy a traditional French crêpe or a casual bistro meal in your hotel's neighborhood (e.g., Le Marais or Latin Quarter).
*   **Afternoon (14:30 - 18:00):** Dive into art at the **Louvre Museum**. Focus on key areas like the Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, and Egyptian antiquities. *Booking tickets in advance is essential to save time.*
*   **Evening (18:30 - 20:00):** Stroll through the beautiful Tuileries Garden, leading towards Place de la Concorde.
*   **Dinner (20:00 onwards):** Explore the vibrant Le Marais district. Enjoy dinner at a charming restaurant offering classic French cuisine. Afterwards, perhaps a drink at a cozy bar.

### Day 2: Impressionist Masterpieces & Parisian Charm

*   **Morning (09:30 - 12:30):** Visit the **Musée d'Orsay**, housed in a former railway station. Marvel at its impressive collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist masterpieces by artists like Monet, Van Gogh, and Renoir. *Pre-book tickets.*
*   **Lunch (12:30 - 13:30):** Grab a quick and delicious sandwich or quiche from a local boulangerie, or dine at a café near the museum.
*   **Afternoon (14:00 - 17:00):** Explore the elegant **Saint-Germain-des-Prés** neighborhood. Browse its independent bookstores, art galleries, and historic cafés (like Café de Flore or Les Deux Magots). Afterwards, relax with a walk through the serene **Luxembourg Gardens**.
*   **Late Afternoon (17:00 - 18:30):** Head towards the Eiffel Tower. Enjoy views from Champ de Mars or Trocadéro.
*   **Dinner (19:30 onwards):** Indulge in a memorable Parisian dinner near the Eiffel Tower or in the Latin Quarter, perhaps trying a classic steak frites. Consider a riverside walk along the Seine.

### Day 3: Bohemian Montmartre & Departure

*   **Morning (09:00 - 12:00):** Immerse yourself in the bohemian charm of **Montmartre**. Walk up to the stunning **Sacré-Cœur Basilica** for panoramic views of Paris. Explore Place du Tertre, where artists create and display their work.
*   **Brunch/Lunch (12:00 - 13:30):** Enjoy a leisurely brunch in Montmartre, or grab a Croque Monsieur from a local café.
*   **Afternoon (14:00 - 16:00):** Depending on your energy and remaining interests, choose one more museum:
    *   **Option A:** **Centre Pompidou** (modern and contemporary art, architecture, and a great view from the top).
    *   **Option B:** **Musée Rodin** (sculptures by Auguste Rodin, including "The Thinker" and "The Kiss," set in a beautiful garden).
*   **Late Afternoon (16:00 - 17:00):** Do some last-minute souvenir shopping or revisit a favorite spot for a final Parisian coffee.
*   **Evening (17:00 onwards):** Make your way back to Gare du Nord for your Eurostar journey back to London.

# 🎒 Travel Tips

1.  **Book in Advance:** Secure your Eurostar tickets, hotel, and museum entry tickets (especially for the Louvre and Musée d'Orsay) well in advance. This saves time, guarantees entry, and often saves money.
2.  **Comfortable Shoes are Key:** You'll be doing a lot of walking! Prioritize comfortable footwear to fully enjoy exploring the city.
3.  **Learn Basic French Phrases:** While many Parisians speak English, attempting a few basic French phrases (hello, goodbye, please, thank you, excuse me) will be appreciated and enhance your experience.
4.  **Utilize the Metro:** The Paris Metro is efficient, extensive, and easy to navigate. Purchase a "carnet" of 10 tickets for convenience and value. Google Maps is excellent for public transport directions.
5.  **Stay Hydrated & Snack Smart:** Carry a reusable water bottle. Grab pastries, fruits, or sandwiches from local bakeries for quick and affordable snacks/lunches.
6.  **Be Aware of Pickpockets:** Especially in crowded tourist areas (Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Metro), be vigilant. Keep your valuables secure and out of sight.
7.  **Consider an eSIM/Local SIM:** For easy navigation and communication, an eSIM or a local SIM card for data will be invaluable.
8.  **Embrace the Parisian Pace:** Don't try to cram too much into each day. Allow yourself to wander, sit at a café, and soak in the atmosphere. It's a solo trip, so enjoy the freedom to follow your own pace.
9.  **Check for Free Walking Tours:** Some companies offer "free" walking tours (tip-based) which are a great way to get an overview of a neighborhood and meet other travelers.
10. **Paris Museum Pass:** For 3 days, with only 2-3 major museums planned, a Paris Museum Pass might not be cost-effective. Calculate if individual tickets are cheaper based on your specific museum choices.
